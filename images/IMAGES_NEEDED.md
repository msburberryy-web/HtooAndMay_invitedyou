# Images for Htoo & May Wedding Invitation
# Amanda Hills, Atsugi · 16 October 2025

---

## HOW THE IMAGES WORK

There are TWO types of images:

1. **Background images** (JPG) — large scene illustrations baked into a single image.
   The whole website background is ONE tall vertical image (`bg-main.jpg`).
   As guests scroll down, they pass through different parts of this image.

2. **PNG cutout images** — transparent background illustrations that float on top
   at the edges of sections (like the dogs, umbrella, chairs in the reference site).

## ─────────────────────────────────────
## TYPE 1 — BACKGROUND IMAGES
## ─────────────────────────────────────

### `bg-main.jpg`
**The entire website background — ONE tall vertical image**
The most important image. All sections sit transparently on top of this.

**Canva canvas size:** Custom — 1440 × 5000px (or 1080 × 3750px for mobile-first)
**Tools:** Canva AI image generation, Adobe Firefly, Midjourney, or DALL-E

**Prompt (Midjourney / Adobe Firefly):**
```
A very tall vertical watercolor illustration for a Japanese autumn mountain wedding website
background. Warm cream/linen (#f5f0e8) base color throughout. The scene flows from top to
bottom showing different heights of the landscape:

TOP (hero area): Pale cream sky with soft watercolor clouds. Distant silhouettes of the
Tanzawa mountain range (Kanagawa). A few Japanese red-crested cranes (tancho) flying in
the far distance. Delicate yellow ginkgo and red maple (momiji) branches frame the top
corners. Yellow vertical watercolor stripe pattern (like wallpaper) visible in the
background — thin stripes, very subtle.

UPPER MIDDLE (ceremony area): Amanda Hills venue building partially visible on a hill,
surrounded by dense autumn foliage — red maple, orange persimmon, golden ginkgo trees.
A winding stone path leads up through the trees. Paper lanterns (chochin) with blue
ribbon bows hang between branches. White doves perch on branches.

LOWER MIDDLE (reception/transport area): The hillside view from above at golden hour —
warm orange and red autumn forest canopy as far as the eye can see. A blue watercolor
ribbon bow decorates a wooden signpost. Sake tokkuri and ochoko cups illustrated as
watercolor props near a rock. A folded gold sensu (Japanese fan) rests open on a mossy
stone. Wedding rings drawn as delicate line art nearby.

BOTTOM (RSVP/footer area): Evening atmosphere. The mountain range silhouette against a
soft sunset sky — peach, soft blue, cream. Fireflies (hotaru) as tiny golden dots.
Autumn maple leaves gently falling. The scene fades softly toward a deeper cream/
linen tone.

OVERALL STYLE: Elegant Japanese watercolor illustration. Loose impressionistic
brushstrokes, paint bleeds visible. Visible paper texture. Romantic and dreamy.
Similar to high-end Japanese wedding stationery. The CENTER of each zone must be
VERY LIGHT (almost white/cream) so text placed over it remains readable.
Props and illustrations should cluster at the LEFT and RIGHT EDGES and at the
TRANSITIONS between sections, not in the center.

Color palette: Cream #f5f0e8, Steel blue #4a6b9e, Autumn red #c0392b,
Golden yellow #e8c840, Sage green #7a9a60, Deep navy #2d4a7a
```

**For Canva users:**
- Create a custom canvas: 1080 × 3600px
- Use Canva's AI "Magic Media" or "Text to Image" with the prompt above
- Alternatively: create 4 separate 1080×900 scenes and merge them vertically in one design
- Keep the center of each section very light/empty for text readability
- Export as high-quality JPG
