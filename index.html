Questions & Answers

How do we get to the venue?

The venue is near Atsugi — roughly 48 minutes from Shinjuku Station. Shuttle buses will operate from Shinjuku, Tokyo Station, and Ikebukuro. As seating is limited to 27–28 guests per bus, please reserve a seat in your RSVP.

Where is the exact location?

We're keeping the exact address a pleasant surprise until after you RSVP! For now, you can plan on traveling to the scenic Atsugi area.

Are children and senior family members welcome?

We would love to celebrate with everyone, but our venue's remote location and limited accessibility may make travel uncomfortable for young children and senior family members. We kindly ask for your understanding.

How should I inform you about dietary requirements?

You can easily note any dietary restrictions or allergies in the RSVP section below under "Dietary requirements".

What time should I arrive?

We recommend arriving about 20 minutes before the 14:30 ceremony so you can settle in comfortably.

Is parking available on site?

Yes, parking is available. Please kindly let us know in advance so we can reserve a space for you.

What is the dress code?

Please refer to the dress code card above.

May I bring a guest (+1)?

If you are attending as a couple, only one RSVP form is needed — simply include your partner's name when filling it out!

Can I update my RSVP details later?

Yes, of course! Plans change, and that's completely okay. If you need to update your attendance, guest details, or transportation preference, simply contact us directly anytime.
